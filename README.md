# 🎙️ Vector Assistant (Android 13 Ready + Gemini Pro AI)
> **Always-On, Screen-Off Voice Control with Speaker Biometrics, Gemini Pro Brain & Bulk Automated WhatsApp Campaigns**

Vector Assistant aapka autonomous AI voice companion hai. Isme Google Gemini Pro connect hai, jo na sirf aam baaton aur sawalon ke jawab deta hai balkay khud **attractive WhatsApp messages draft** kar ke aapke contacts ko send bhi kar sakta hai!

---

## 🌟 Top 2 Advanced Superpowers

### 1. 🧠 Google Gemini Pro Integration (Smart Brain)
* **Status:** App settings mein direct apni Gemini Pro API key enter karein.
* **Fayda:**
  * Vector robot jaisi awaz ki bajaye ek intehayi intelligent insaan ki tarah baat karega.
  * Urdu aur English dono mein lambi aur gehari guftugu kar sakta hai.
  * Maslan: *"Vector, stock market ka trend kaisa hai?"* ya *"Physics mein relativity kya hai?"*

### 2. 🤖 Multi-Contact WhatsApp Campaign (Attractive Auto-Drafting)
* **Command:**
  > *"Vector, in sab ko WhatsApp per attractive message bhejo: Ali, Hamza, Bilal ke Hamare restaurant par 30% discount shuru ho gaya hai"*
* **Kya Hoga?**
  1. **Gemini Pro Drafting:** Gemini foran ek professional, engaging aur emojis ke sath khoobsurat message likhega.
  2. **Automated Sending:** Vector khud baari baari sabhi contacts ke numbers dhoond kar WhatsApp chat khol kar message send karega.
  3. **Anti-Ban Safety Delay:** Har contact ke darmiyan 3.5 seconds ka natural gap rakha gaya hai taake WhatsApp account par koi ban ya spam restriction na lage!

## 🔒 Voice Biometrics (Sirf Aapki Awaz)
App screen par **"Record Voice Sample (0/3)"** par click karein aur 3 baar bolen: *"Hey Vector, Suno Vector"*. Vector aapki awaz lock kar lega, taake koi doosra aapke phone par hukam na chala sake!

## 📲 App Setup (Sirf 4 Quick Steps):
1. **Screen-Off Run:** Battery settings mein app ko *"Unrestricted"* karein.
2. **Phone Automation:** Accessibility Services mein "Vector Assistant" ko **ON** karein.
3. **Calls & Call Logs:** "Allow" karein.
4. **VIP Notifications:** Notification Access mein Vector ko **Allow** karein.
5. **Gemini Pro:** App mein apni Gemini API key paste kar ke **"CONNECT GEMINI PRO"** dabayein.
